## 2. Hosting and technical access data

This website is hosted by Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestraße 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany. When pages are requested, Hetzner processes technical data, including IP address, request time, requested address, referrer, browser, operating system and error information.

This processing enables website delivery, troubleshooting and protection against abuse. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is the secure and reliable operation of the website. A data processing agreement is in place with Hetzner.

Regular web server logs are generally deleted after 14 days. Security incidents may require longer retention for investigation and legal claims.

Further information: [Hetzner privacy policy](https://www.hetzner.com/legal/privacy-policy/).

## 3. Cookies, analytics and embedded content

The website itself does not set cookies or save preferences in local storage or session storage. We do not use analytics or marketing services, tracking pixels, embedded videos or social media plugins. Fonts, images, stylesheets and JavaScript are served with the website.

The interactive movie examples run entirely in your browser. They do not access your media files, upload files or connect to an AI provider.

## 4. Contact by email or phone

When you contact us, we process your contact details, the content of your enquiry and the communication data needed to respond. Email is handled through the mail servers of our Hetzner web hosting service.

For enquiries relating to a contract, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Other enquiries are processed under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is responding to your request. Ordinary enquiries are deleted six months after they have been resolved, unless a contractual relationship, statutory retention requirements or necessary records require longer storage.

## 5. External links

The website links in particular to the OpenMemento repository on GitHub and to marcogriep.de. These services are not embedded. Your browser contacts the respective service when you open a link; its own privacy notice then applies. Issues, contributions and attachments published on GitHub may be publicly visible.

Further information: [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) and [marcogriep.de privacy policy](https://marcogriep.de/datenschutz/).

## 6. Separate desktop application

This is an informational website. Visiting it does not start video analysis or send family videos or API keys to Google Gemini. Use of the separately installed desktop application and any AI analysis enabled there is independent of visiting this website. Your use of the Gemini API is also subject to the terms and privacy information of your Google service.

## 7. Recipients, retention and security

The hosting and email providers described above receive data as necessary to provide their services. We otherwise disclose data only where permitted by a legal basis or required by law. Transfers to third countries are not intended for the hosting and email services we use.

We retain personal data only as long as needed for its purpose or to meet legal obligations. Specific deletion periods stated above take precedence. Website traffic is encrypted using HTTPS with TLS.

## 8. Your rights

Subject to the applicable legal conditions, you have the following rights:

- Access to your personal data (Article 15 GDPR).
- Rectification of inaccurate data (Article 16 GDPR).
- Erasure and restriction of processing (Articles 17 and 18 GDPR).
- Data portability (Article 20 GDPR).
- Withdrawal of consent with effect for the future (Article 7(3) GDPR).

**Right to object:** Where processing is based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, you may object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Article 21 GDPR). You may object to direct marketing at any time without giving reasons.

To exercise your rights, contact us using the details above. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Article 77 GDPR), in particular the [State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Rhineland-Palatinate](https://www.datenschutz.rlp.de/).
