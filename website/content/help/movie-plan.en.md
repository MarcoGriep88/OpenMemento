## Generate a selection

Under **Movie & export**, open **Create / edit movie plan**. **Generate selection / replan** builds a plan from saved analyses for your project's footage and selected date range.

The plan references precise time ranges in the source files. A long recording can contain several interesting moments, so selection is not limited to entire files.

## Review events and segments

Check that important events are represented, similar shots are not repeated too often and segment boundaries make sense. Use the segment preview and full movie preview. Pay particular attention to the beginning and end of speech and reactions.

Editing includes excluding segments, including important moments, changing boundaries and adjusting event importance. These changes do not cut the original files or alter their stored analysis.

## Adjust length and importance

Target duration is a guideline. If too little suitable footage is available, the movie will be shorter. Give an important event more weight and replan when needed. Review the result again because replanning changes the selection.

## Preview and editing state

Use **Undo** and **Redo** for supported plan changes. Save the movie plan or project state before stopping work. Preview again after changing footage, date range or settings.

> The goal is a good first cut. Sharpness, movement or an AI rating alone cannot determine what matters to your family.
