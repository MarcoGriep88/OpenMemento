## 1. Start with a small collection

Try a few recordings from a trip or birthday. Keep the originals on their drive and maintain a separate backup of your recordings. OpenMemento reads source files without modifying them.

## 2. Create a movie project

In the dashboard, choose **+ Create new movie**. Give it a name and target duration. The additional Gemini prompt is optional; leave it empty for your first attempt. Create the empty project and continue to its footage.

![Movie project in OpenMemento](/images/app-project-en.png)

## 3. Add and index footage

Under **Footage & indexing**, use **Add folder** to select one or more media folders. Start **Index project footage**. Check recording dates afterwards and optionally restrict the date range.

## 4. Analyze the moments

Under **OpenCV & Gemini**, run local OpenCV analysis first. Then configure your own Gemini key in settings. **Prepare Gemini analysis** shows the planned scope. Analysis videos with audio are sent to Google only after you approve that step.

## 5. Review the first cut

Choose an editing style under **Editing & music**, optionally adding local background music. Under **Movie & export**, open **Create / edit movie plan**. Generate a selection and review its events, segments and preview. Adjust unsuitable moments before exporting.

## 6. Export locally

In the movie dialog, choose **Export movie locally as MP4** and select a new output file. Wait for the job to finish, then watch the result.
